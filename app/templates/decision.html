How to read this

Major Overperformer: production is well above ratings-based expectation. Could be a system fit, hidden gem, or lineup beneficiary.

Major Underperformer: ratings suggest more value than the player is producing. Check role, usage, stamina, lineup context, and shot profile.

The Expected BPR formula is intentionally simple for now: (Best Role Score - 50) / 4. Once enough seasons are imported, this can be replaced with a learned regression based on your actual Hoops Dynasty data.
